Questions

Where can I get Overlode?

Overlode is on Google Play for Android. An iPhone version isn't available yet.

Is Overlode free?

Yes. Logging, progression targets, history, graphs and cloud sync are free. Overlode Pro adds the weight stack scanner, gym profiles and weekly insights.

What if I've had time off?

Tap Been away? on a session and enter how many weeks it's been. Overlode eases your targets to suit, and builds back from there.

Will there be an AI coach?

Yes, as a future Pro feature. Tell it your goals and it builds your sessions and reviews your progress.

What happens to my data?

Your training is stored on your phone, and copied to your account only if you sign in. There are no ads and your data is never sold. Read the privacy policy.
