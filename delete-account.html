Delete your account

Overlode workout tracker

You can delete your Overlode account and the data stored with it at any time, either in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Overlode and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and its cloud data are deleted straight away.

Have Overlode Pro? Deleting your account doesn't cancel the subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play: Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Overlode.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email overloadapp@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted. It will be deleted within 30 days and you will get a reply once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email address and account ID).
  • Everything synced to your account: sessions, workouts, sets, bodyweight entries, gym profiles, machine weights and goals.
  • On request by email, your purchase history held by RevenueCat.

What is kept

  • Workouts stored on your phone stay there until you remove them with Settings > Danger zone > Delete all workout data, or uninstall the app.
  • Records of Google Play purchases, which Google and RevenueCat keep as required for tax and accounting.
  • Anonymous crash reports and usage statistics are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically by Firebase under Firebase's standard retention periods.

Only want to delete some data?

Delete all workout data in Settings > Danger zone clears your workouts without deleting your account.
