Overlode Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overlode is a workout tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and how to remove it.

What stays on your phone

Your sessions, workouts, sets, notes, bodyweight entries, personal records, targets, gym profiles, goals and settings are stored on your device. You can use Overlode as a guest without an account, in which case this data stays on your phone only (apart from Android's own device backup, if you have it turned on).

Optional account and cloud sync

You can create an Overlode account with an email address and password. Accounts are provided by Google Firebase Authentication, which also sends account emails such as password resets.

While you are signed in, your sessions, workouts, bodyweight entries, gym profiles (including the machine weights you save) and goals are copied to your account in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore. This keeps them backed up and in sync across your devices. Only your account can read or change this data. Signing out stops syncing and leaves your data on the phone.

Overlode Pro

Overlode Pro is not on sale yet. When it is, it will be a subscription bought through Google Play. Google Play will handle payment, and Overlode never sees your card or payment details. Purchases will be verified and managed by RevenueCat, which will receive your Overlode account ID, your purchase details (product, price, dates and status) and basic device and app information, so Pro can be unlocked on every phone you sign in to. RevenueCat will process this on Overlode's behalf (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

If your account has Overlode Pro, a record saying so may also be stored with your account in Firebase. The app can read it but cannot change it.

You can cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

Camera and photos

The weight stack scanner (a Pro feature) uses a photo you take or choose to read the numbers on a machine's weight stack. The text is read on your phone using Google ML Kit. Your photo is not uploaded and is deleted from the app's temporary storage once it has been read. ML Kit may send Google anonymous information about how the feature performs, but not your photos or what they show.

Crash reports and analytics

Overlode uses Firebase Crashlytics to send crash reports and Firebase Analytics to send anonymous usage statistics, so crashes can be found and fixed and the app improved. These may include your device model, Android version, app version and how the app was used at the time. The advertising ID is not collected.

What Overlode does not do

Overlode does not show advertising, does not sell your data, and does not share it with anyone except the Google Firebase services and RevenueCat described above, which process it on Overlode's behalf.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings > Danger zone lets you:
- Delete your account, which deletes your login and the copy of your data in the cloud. Data on your phone stays.
- Delete all workout data, which removes your data from the phone.

You can also export a backup of your data at any time from Settings > Backup & restore.
